About us

One family, two properties

Snuggle Inn Boarding Kennels, 459 Fernside Rd, Rangiora, is owned and operated by Snuggle Inn Boarding Cattery, 166 Giles Rd, Kaiapoi.

The cattery & kennels are one of the largest boarding facilities in the South Island. Both are operated to the same high standard on separate properties.
